OFFICIAL GUIDELINES

Rules & Technical Specifications

Official guidelines formulated by the Government of West Bengal.

Rule 01 • Formats

Formats & Resolutions

Video reels: 9:16 vertical (1080×1920), 30/60fps, 30–60s (Shankhadhwani: 20–60s single-take). Static photos: high-res 3:4 or 3:2. Sub-720p or blurry content is disqualified.

Rule 02 • Eligibility

Age 16+ & Shot in West Bengal

Open to Indian nationals, NRIs, OCIs and visiting tourists. Creators must be 16+ as of 1 Oct 2026 (under-18s need parental consent). All content must be shot within West Bengal.

Rule 03 • Clean Media

Clean Media & Zero AI

Use the Instagram audio library or festival ambient audio. No third-party watermarks (CapCut, TikTok), no commercial overlays. Generative-AI and archival stock footage are permanently banned.

Rule 04 • RAW & EXIF

RAW Masters + EXIF Proof

Retain original camera master files with timestamped EXIF data. Finalists must submit high-res masters via Google Drive within 24 hours of notification, or forfeit their prize.

COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the official Durga Puja Content Creation Competition 2026.

What is the age limit to enter the competition?

Participants must be at least 16 years of age as of 1 October 2026. Entrants aged 16–17 must submit written parent or legal guardian consent. Individuals under 16 as of 1 October 2026 are not eligible to participate.

Who can enter the competition?

The competition is open to Indian nationals, Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs), and foreign tourists visiting West Bengal. All submitted media must be shot within the state of West Bengal during 1–23 October 2026.

Can I use CapCut, third-party templates, or watermarked apps?

No. Submitted content must not contain third-party app watermarks, brand overlays, or template credits (such as CapCut or TikTok). Audio should be from the Instagram audio library or original festival ambient sound.

What if my Instagram or Facebook profile is private?

Entries from private profiles cannot be verified and are disqualified. Your profile must remain public throughout the contest window, and you must follow and tag the official handle @durgapujawb.

What are RAW files and why must I keep them?

RAW files are the original, uncompressed camera master files containing intact timestamped EXIF metadata. They verify that the media was genuinely captured by you in West Bengal between 1–23 October 2026.

What happens if I miss the 24-hour RAW submission window?

Shortlisted finalists must upload their uncompressed RAW master files with EXIF metadata via Google Drive within 24 hours of notification, or they forfeit their prize.

Can I submit multiple entries?

Please contact support@durgapujawb.com.

What is the official submission deadline?

The competition closes strictly on 23 October 2026 at 11:59 PM IST*. Late submissions will not be accepted or evaluated.

*Deadline subject to confirmation
Can I use generative AI imagery or stock footage?

No. There is zero tolerance for generative AI imagery, stock footage, or archival footage from previous years. Any such content will result in immediate permanent disqualification.

Do creators retain ownership of their content?

Yes, 100%. Creators retain full intellectual property copyright over their creative work. Winning participants grant the Government of West Bengal and Calcutta Instagrammers a royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to feature their work with creator credit.

Have an additional query? Contact our official helpdesk at support@durgapujawb.com.

Official Competition Terms & Conditions

1. Contest Authority: The “Durga Puja Content Creation Competition 2026” is an official initiative by the Government of West Bengal.

2. Contest Window: The competition opens on 1 October 2026 and closes on 23 October 2026 at 11:59 PM IST*. Late submissions will not be considered.

*Deadline subject to confirmation

3. Eligibility: Open to Indian nationals, NRIs, OCIs, and foreign tourists in West Bengal during the window. Entrants must be 16 years or older as of 1 October 2026. Participants under 18 must possess written parental/guardian consent. All content must be recorded within the state of West Bengal.

4. Intellectual Property & Copyright: Creators retain 100% intellectual property copyright over their submitted work. By submitting, winners grant the Government of West Bengal a royalty-free, non-exclusive license to feature and broadcast their submissions across digital, social, broadcast, and print media with creator credit.

5. Authenticity, RAW Proof & Anti-Fraud: Plagiarism, use of stock/archival footage from previous years, and generative AI imagery will result in immediate permanent disqualification. Finalists must provide uncompressed master files with EXIF metadata within 24 hours of notification via Google Drive.

6. Prize Structure & Adjudication: Total prize pool is ₹4,00,000 distributed equally across 4 categories (₹50,000 / ₹30,000 / ₹20,000 per category). Evaluation is conducted by an independent Government-Expert Grand Jury (Originality 35%, Theme Fit 30%, Craft 20%, Reach 15%). The Grand Jury’s verdict is final and binding.

7. Jurisdiction & Governance: Any legal disputes arising out of the competition shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the High Court at Calcutta. Official support email: support@durgapujawb.com.
