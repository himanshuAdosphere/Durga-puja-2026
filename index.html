DURGA PUJA 2026

A Bigger, Brighter Durga Puja

Every year we welcome Maa Durga. This year, we welcome her fuller — with cleaner streets, louder conch shells and a burst of creativity from every corner of Bengal.

The idea is simple and ancient: Maa's ten hands each hold a power — Strength, Progress, Awakening, Courage, Focus, Confidence, Resolve, Prosperity, Protection and Ambition. Together they are Dasha Shakti, the complete power of Bengal. The Dasha Shakti Challenge invites you to carry one of those powers into the world and share it.

“Maa is coming. Let's welcome her with cleanliness, creativity and community.”

PARTICIPATION GUIDELINES

Before You Participate

Essential guidelines to ensure your creative submission is officially recognized and celebrated across Bengal.

Rule 01

100% Original Content

Your reel or video must be genuinely created, filmed and conceptualized by you. Avoid copyrighted third-party media or clips.

Rule 02

Theme Alignment

Anchor your entry strictly in the chosen track: expressing Maa's 10 sacred symbols, community swachhta, or blowing the conch shell.

Rule 03

Public Social Visibility

Your post and social profile must remain public on Instagram, YouTube, or Facebook so our jury can access and verify your link.

Rule 04

Multiple Entries Welcome

Submit entries across different challenges! You can participate in Creative Reels, Swachhta, and the Shankha challenge simultaneously.

Fair Cultural Jury & Civic Integrity

Every eligible entry is evaluated impartially by an independent cultural committee celebrating authenticity, creative craft, and civic impact.

Dasha Shakti 2026 — Contest Terms & Conditions

1. Eligibility: Open to all creators, residents, and cultural enthusiasts across Bengal and globally. No registration fee is required.

2. Originality & Ownership: Submissions must be entirely original work. Any plagiarism or infringement of intellectual property will lead to disqualification.

3. Content Guidelines: All videos and media must remain respectful, culturally honoring, and celebratory of Bengal’s heritage. Defamatory, unsafe, or offensive material is strictly prohibited.

4. Public Visibility: The submitted reel, post, or video must remain public on Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube during the campaign evaluation period.

5. Adjudication: Entries will be evaluated by an independent cultural and creative jury based on creativity, community impact, cultural relevance, and adherence to the challenge theme. The jury’s decision is final.
