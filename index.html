Competition Terms & Conditions

1. Contest Authority: The “Durga Puja West Bengal — Creators Contest” is a statewide creative initiative.

2. Contest Window: The competition runs during the festival window. Late submissions will not be considered.

3. Eligibility: Open to Indian nationals, NRIs, OCIs, and foreign tourists in West Bengal. Entrants must be 16 years or older as of 1 October 2026. Participants under 18 must possess written parental/guardian consent. All content must be recorded within the state of West Bengal.

4. Intellectual Property & Copyright: Creators retain 100% intellectual property copyright over their submitted work. By submitting, participants grant a royalty-free, non-exclusive license to feature and broadcast their submissions across digital and social channels with creator credit.

5. Authenticity: Plagiarism and generative AI imagery will result in immediate disqualification.

6. Jurisdiction & Governance: Any legal disputes arising out of the competition shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the High Court at Calcutta. Official support email: support@durgapujawb.com.
