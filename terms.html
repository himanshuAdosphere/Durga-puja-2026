Section 01

Eligibility Criteria

1.1 Age Requirement: All participants must be 16 years of age or older as of 1 October 2026. Entrants under the age of 16 as of 1 October 2026 are strictly prohibited from participating.

1.2 Guardian Consent for Minors: Participants aged 16 and 17 as of 1 October 2026 are legally required to provide parent/legal guardian consent (full name and verified consent declaration) on the official submission form.

1.3 Nationality & Residency: The competition is open to Indian nationals, Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs), and international tourists currently present in the State of West Bengal.

1.4 Geographical Capture: All submitted entries must be freshly filmed within the geographic boundaries of the State of West Bengal during the competition window.

Section 02

Technical Rules & Specifications

2.1 Media Formats & Resolution:

  • Video Reels: 9:16 vertical ratio (1080×1920 pixels), 30fps or 60fps. Sub-720p or blurry content will be disqualified.

2.2 Social Accounts & Tagging: Posts must be hosted on public Instagram or Facebook accounts owned by the creator. Private profiles are disqualified. Entrants must follow and tag the official handle @durgapujawb.

2.3 Audio & Clean Branding: Entrants should use tracks from the official Instagram audio library or original festival ambient recordings. No copyrighted commercial songs that trigger copyright strikes. No third-party watermarks, brand overlays, or template credits (such as CapCut or TikTok) are allowed.

2.4 Master RAW Files & EXIF Metadata: Finalists must retain uncompressed camera master files with intact EXIF timestamp and metadata. Finalists must submit these masters via Google Drive within 24 hours of notification.

2.5 Anti-Plagiarism & Zero AI Policy: Generative AI imagery, AI-synthesized videos, stock footage, or archival content from past editions are strictly prohibited. Plagiarism results in an immediate permanent ban.

2.6 Submission Deadline & Close Time: All entries must be posted publicly and submitted via the official portal by the announced deadline. Late submissions will not be considered or evaluated.

Section 03

Judging Framework & Weightage

3.1 Grand Jury Composition: Evaluation is conducted by an independent Expert Grand Jury composed of distinguished cultural historians, visual artists, and digital media curators.

3.2 Four Pillars of Weightage:

  • Originality & Story (35%): Fresh cultural angle, creative perspective, and narrative emotional depth.
  • Theme Alignment (30%): Fidelity to the chosen competition category guidelines and Bengal festive heritage.
  • Technical Craft (20%): Composition, visual balance, lighting, color grading, audio clarity, and pacing.
  • Organic Reach & Resonance (15%): Authentic audience interaction and positive community engagement. Engagement manipulation (bots, click farms) will result in disqualification.

3.3 Finality of Verdict: The verdict of the Expert Grand Jury is final, conclusive, and not subject to appeal.

Section 04

Intellectual Property (IP) & Licensing

4.1 100% Creator IP Ownership: Creators retain 100% intellectual property copyright over their creative work at all times. Entrants represent and warrant that they are the sole creators and copyright owners of all submitted media.

4.2 Non-Exclusive Promotional Licence: By winning or being shortlisted, participants grant the Durga Puja West Bengal — Creators Contest a royalty-free, non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide licence to broadcast, display, reproduce, and feature their entries across digital portals, social media channels, and cultural campaigns, with prominent creator credit.

4.3 Third-Party Rights & Indemnity: Entrants ensure that their media does not violate any third-party privacy, likeness, or property rights.

Section 05

Governance, Jurisdiction & Helpdesk

5.1 Legal Jurisdiction: Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to the competition shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the High Court at Calcutta.

5.2 Awards & Statutory Compliance: Official certificates and creator recognition will be awarded across categories. Tax deduction at source (TDS) shall apply as per prevailing regulations where applicable.

5.3 Official Helpdesk: For any inquiries, official communications, or questions, contact the dedicated helpdesk:
Email: support@durgapujawb.com
